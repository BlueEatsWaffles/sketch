# Sketchbook Menu

A hand-drawn, notebook-style mod menu. Press **Tab** to open it.

- **Bot**: macro bot and replay bot (with frame fixes), macro library, frame stepper
- **Video Maker**: FFmpeg renderer (best quality + music) or Built-in renderer (fast, nothing to install)
- **Hitboxes & hit borders**: colors per object type, thickness, fill, trail, show on death
- **Speedhack**, FPS unlock, lock delta
- **Noclip**, safe mode, respawn options, jump hack, auto clicker and more
- **Labels**: FPS, CPS, noclip accuracy, attempts, clock, cheat indicator...
- **Themes**: 9 themes plus your own custom colors, with smooth animations

FFmpeg renderer: install FFmpeg (free) and the menu's Setup report will find it.
